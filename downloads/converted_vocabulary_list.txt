artefact - an old human-made object - The museum displayed an ancient artefact from Egypt.
deteriorate - to become worse - The old bridge began to deteriorate after many years.
vernacular - common local language - English is my vernacular language.
government - group that runs a country - The government announced new rules for schools.

miraculous - like a miracle - His recovery was almost miraculous.
relish - to enjoy greatly - I relish the taste of fresh mangoes.
ethically - in a morally right way - The company works ethically in all its deals.
humanitarian - helping people in need - She does humanitarian work for poor children.
beacon - guiding light or signal - The lighthouse acted as a beacon for ships.
philanthropy - helping others by giving money/support - He is known for his philanthropy.
mortified - extremely embarrassed - She was mortified after forgetting her speech.
sustainability - using resources so they last long - Sustainability is important for protecting the
environment.

characteristics - special features/qualities - Good leaders have many strong characteristics.
persistent - continuing without stopping - His persistent effort helped him succeed.
occurrence - something that happens - Earthquakes are a natural occurrence in some areas.
scorching - extremely hot - The desert heat was scorching.
dearth - shortage or lack - There is a dearth of clean water in some regions.
